PomoFomo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PomoFomo is a Chrome browser extension that helps children take regular breaks from the screen. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

Information we collect

PomoFomo does not collect, transmit, store on any server, or share any personal information. The developer never receives any data from the extension.

To work, the extension keeps the following data in Chrome’s own extension storage:

  • Timer settings (focus and break lengths, rounds before a long break, character, sounds, nag mode, resume after restart), plus an optional first name you can enter so the character can say hi. Leave it blank and no name is stored.
  • Timer state (current phase, time remaining, round number, whether the one-per-round snooze has been used)
  • A session log of finished focus rounds and breaks: when each started and ended, whether it ran its full length, and whether the child left a break early. It is used only to show the “This week” stats. The most recent 500 entries are kept; older ones are deleted automatically.
  • The bookcase: one entry per full break (the kind of reward earned, such as a book, and when). It is kept until the extension is removed, so the bookcase can keep filling up.
  • Whether the first-run welcome screen has been seen.

Timer state, the session log, the bookcase and the welcome flag stay on your device. Timer settings are saved with Chrome sync, so if you are signed in to Chrome with sync turned on, Chrome copies them to your other computers through your Google account, the same way it syncs other extension settings. This includes the optional first name, if you entered one. That copy is handled by Google under Google’s Privacy Policy; it never passes through the developer. With Chrome sync off, settings stay on your device.

Checking for tab switches during breaks

During a break, PomoFomo notices when a different browser tab is brought to the front. It checks only whether that tab is PomoFomo’s own timer tab. If it isn’t, the break is marked as left early (a single yes/no) and the extension shows one reminder notification.

PomoFomo does not record, store or send which tab or website was opened, its address, its title or its contents. Outside of breaks, tab switches are ignored.

Permissions used

PomoFomo requests the following Chrome permissions solely to operate the timer. No permission is used to send data anywhere.

  • alarms — to advance the timer reliably when the browser is idle
  • storage — to save settings (synced by Chrome as described above), timer state, the session log and the bookcase
  • tabs — to open, pin, and manage the dedicated timer tab, bring it to the front when a break starts, and check whether a tab brought to the front during a break is the timer tab
  • notifications — for the heads-up before a break, the break alert, and a reminder if a break is left early

Third-party services

PomoFomo makes no network requests and uses no third-party services, analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. Its fonts, pictures and sounds are all bundled inside the extension.

Children's privacy

PomoFomo is designed for use by children under parental supervision. Because no data is ever sent to the developer or any third party, it complies with COPPA (the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and similar regulations.

Deleting your data

Removing PomoFomo from Chrome deletes its timer state, session log, bookcase and settings from that computer. You can clear the optional name at any time in the extension’s settings. Settings synced through Chrome sync can be cleared from your Google account in Chrome’s sync settings.

Changes to this policy

If the extension is ever updated in a way that affects data handling, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date will change.
